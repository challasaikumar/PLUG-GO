## Market pattern references

Current Indian charging-network sites were reviewed as examples of expected driver capabilities: live availability, compatible connector filters, route planning, booking, QR start, payment, session status, invoice, reviews, host access, and support. These examples informed UX expectations, not regulatory claims.

## Important limits

- State/UT EV policy, DISCOM tariff, grant/tender, property, safety and tax obligations can differ. Confirm the customer’s actual legal/compliance requirements with its State Nodal Agency, DISCOM, payment provider and counsel.
- OCPP support is charger-model and firmware specific. Validate with a physical test charger before committing to public live-control dates.
- No source guarantees search ranking, uptime, charging availability or safety. All public claims should be evidence-backed and dated.
