# Local SEO and Google Business Profile checklist

Use this for **each live Plug and Go station listing**. The website catalogue is the source of truth for name, address, hours, and phone. Do not invent details to win a map pack.

## Exact NAP and hours consistency

For every station, the **same** values must appear on:

1. The published Plug and Go station page
2. Google Business Profile (GBP)
3. Any other map or directory listing the operator controls
4. On-site signage, if present

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Business name | Exact brand + site name as published (no keyword stuffing, no “Best EV charger Hyderabad”) |
| Address | Same line 1, locality, city, state, pincode as the catalogue |
| Phone | Only a published support or site number. Do not use a personal mobile “for ranking” |
| Hours | Same as `accessHoursSummary` / structured hours. If unknown, do not guess 24/7 |
| Website | Canonical station URL (`/stations/[state]/[city]/[slug]`) |
| Category | EV charging station / equivalent — not an unrelated category |

If the catalogue says hours are unknown, the listing must not claim 24/7.

## Station pin and arrival-photo checklist

Before marking a listing live:

- [ ] Pin is on the **bay or entrance the driver should use**, not the mall centroid if that sends people to the wrong gate
- [ ] Lat/lng on GBP match the published station coordinates (or a documented, reviewed correction)
- [ ] Cover photo is an approved arrival / entrance photo with accurate alt intent (what the driver will see)
- [ ] Additional photos: bay, connector (without implying a type that is not installed), wayfinding
- [ ] No stock photos of other networks or other cities
- [ ] Photos are owned or licensed; do not scrape competitor listings

Re-check the pin after a site change (new ramp, closed entrance, relocated bays).

## Special-hours update workflow

Owner: **station listing owner** (named below) within **one business day** of a known change.

1. Update the catalogue hours (and special-hours note) first
2. Update GBP special hours (festivals, maintenance, temporary closure)
3. Confirm the public station page no longer contradicts the listing
4. If the site is unsafe or offline, do not leave “open” on the listing

Do not leave festival hours stale. Review at least **every 90 days** even if nothing changed.

## Real review request and response process

- Ask for a review only after a **real visit**, without incentives that violate Google’s policies
- Share the official listing link; do not script five-star wording
- Respond to reviews from the official owner account, factually, without inventing uptime or prices
- Off-platform complaints still go through Support; do not argue privately in a way that contradicts the public page
- **Never buy reviews, fake reviews, or review gating that hides poor scores**

## Correcting incorrect map or listing data

1. Compare the listing to the published station page
2. File an edit in GBP / Maps with evidence (photo of signage, catalogue screenshot)
3. Log the request date and listing URL in the station’s internal notes
4. If a third party created a duplicate, mark it as duplicate; do not create a second “SEO” listing
5. Do not pay agencies to build fake citations or to “guarantee rank”

## Never buy reviews or backlinks

- No paid 5-star packages, PBNs, or directory spam
- No automated guest-post networks targeting “EV charging in [city]”
- Link only to real partners, regulators, and pages that match visible facts

## Required owner and review frequency

| Station listing | Named owner (role) | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Each published station GBP | Station operator or assigned content manager | **Monthly** pin/hours/photo check; **90-day** full NAP review |
| Duplicate / unofficial listings | Same owner | **Monthly** until resolved |
| Special hours / outages | Same owner + support | **Same day** when operations know |

Record the owner name in the station internal notes. If no owner is assigned, the listing is not ready for public promotion.

Replace this table’s owner names when the Plug and Go operations team assigns them. Do not publish fictional owners on the website.
